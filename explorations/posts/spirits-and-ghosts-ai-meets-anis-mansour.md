Many of us grew up reading the novels of **Anis Mansour**. Through his writing he transported us to a world filled with spirits and ghosts, and set our imaginations alight.

**"Spirits and Ghosts"**, named after his novel, uses AI to portray that world. It combines mysticism and technology to capture the spirits' haunting essence: glowing fairy-tale characters set against beautiful surroundings, each spirit carrying its own traits and life story.

3D animation then lets the audience step inside this realm of the unknown, with spectral characters and settings that could not be filmed.
