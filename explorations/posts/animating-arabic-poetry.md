We are developing **3D animated episodes** that show the different forms of Arabic poetry and the remarkable ways they were used.

The main subjects the series covers:

- **Wars, raids and the great poetry duels**
- **The old trade and pilgrimage routes**, and the places they reached
- **Describing cities** and celebrating their genealogies
- **Poems of love and praise**, for a beloved, a homeland or a great figure in Arab history

The poetic lines and rhetorical ideas are given a graphic treatment, so the meaning becomes clearer and the language feels closer to today's audience, in our own beautiful Arabic.
